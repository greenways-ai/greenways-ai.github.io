Greenways research programme

The questions connecting our work on collaborative AI, participatory markets and software infrastructure.

Greenways·Programme note 001·Living document

Communities create futures that do not yet exist.

A company, a research group, a local community and an online network all face a similar problem. Their future cannot be produced by one person acting alone. It emerges through shared belief, accumulated knowledge, coordinated work and repeated decisions.

Most software helps individuals produce outputs. Less software helps groups preserve the context around those outputs, understand how they relate, and turn participation into durable collective capability.

We are interested in the point where participation becomes infrastructure.

Three connected research areas

1. Collaborative intelligence

How can conversation, spatial arrangement, documentation and specialised AI capabilities operate as one continuous environment? Greenways Spaces investigates how teams can move from uncertain exploration to structured knowledge without losing provenance or context.

2. Visible participation

Likes and impressions are weak representations of belief. Statstrade explores richer mechanisms: forecasts, commitments, earned standing and social signals that sit between passive attention and formal ownership.

3. Application infrastructure

Interfaces change quickly; data and workflows endure. Our technical work examines database-backed application slices, generated RPC and type metadata, event-driven state, synchronisation, and shared models that can serve web, mobile and edge applications.

Working principles

  • Participation over attention: design for contribution and consequence, not only engagement.
  • Artifacts over outputs: preserve generated work as versioned, reusable and attributable objects.
  • Shared context over isolated sessions: make relationships, decisions and origins visible.
  • Standing through participation: let reputation emerge from useful action over time.
  • Infrastructure before interface: define robust data and workflows before multiplying surfaces.

How this library will develop

The papers published here will range from technical architecture notes to product theses and longer social arguments. Some will be stable publications. Others will remain living documents that evolve as systems are implemented and tested.

The purpose is not to separate research from product work. It is to make the assumptions behind the products visible enough to challenge, refine and reuse.
