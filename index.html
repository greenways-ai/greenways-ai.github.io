CONNECTED WORLDS

Build experiences that can meet each other.

A world can be a game, a community, a studio, a service, or something nobody has named yet. Greenways is about making each one easier to build and ready to connect.

01

Build the experience

Start with the thing people see, use, and care about. The world should feel complete before the infrastructure gets in the way.

02

Connect what matters

Bring identity, content, events, tools, and communities together without forcing every world into the same shape.

03

Keep room to grow

Begin with one useful experience. Add new places, people, and capabilities when the world is ready for them.

OPEN STANDARDS

Build once. Connect when you are ready.

Greenways standards are designed to help worlds exchange identity, content, events, and state without being absorbed into one platform. Your experience keeps its own character while gaining ways to work with others.

Portable identity

People should not have to become a different person in every world.

Shared language

Tools and services can cooperate without becoming identical.

Builder control

You decide what connects, what stays local, and how the world evolves.

FOR BUILDERS OF EVERY KIND
